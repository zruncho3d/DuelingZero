### What is necessary here?

The bare minimum is just the feet and the rear motor cover+mount. These are all based on the `Desune` CAD file, but some modified for a more simple/general build. The rear power skirt is also modifier to utilize the stock V0.2 AC adapter rather than the different one Desune uses.

For filling out the skirt, the necessary quantities of each part are printed.

The 'side_skirt_no_fan' can be swapped for 'side_fan_skirt' if you prefer to skip on extra ventilation and still want to have those areas closed off.


These are also compatibile with the floor panel that `Desune`  used in their iteration to protect the electronics bay.
